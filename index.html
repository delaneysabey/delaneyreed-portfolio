04 / About

About

Hi! I’m Delaney, a technical artist and developer working at DreamWorks Animation. I studied computer science and animation at BYU, where I discovered that my favorite part of making animated films is figuring out how to make them work.

I love creating tools for artists and solving the weird technical problems that come with bringing animated stories to life. When I’m not working, I enjoy making my own animated projects, learning something new, playing guitar, listening to music, watching movies, running, or finding a steal at the thrift shop.
