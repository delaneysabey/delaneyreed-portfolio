Nolan & Nelson

Personal project / 0:15-0:25 / Blender, Python

Nolan & Nelson is an ongoing solo animated web series. I handle the full 3D production pipeline: sculpting and modeling the dinosaur characters, building and rigging them, creating environments, animating shots, and editing the final videos.
