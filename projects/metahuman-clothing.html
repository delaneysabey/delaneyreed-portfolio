Custom MetaHuman Clothing

Ancient Jerusalem recreation / 0:43-0:50 / Unreal Engine, Marvelous Designer, Maya

As a student employee for the BYU Religion Department, I contributed to a virtual recreation of ancient Jerusalem by creating MetaHuman characters and custom clothing.

I created garments in Marvelous Designer and textured them in Substance Painter and Substance Designer, then bound them to the MetaHuman skeleton in Maya, refined skin weights, and integrated the characters into Unreal Engine.
