DNEG Internship

Midas Cinematic / 0:50-0:59 / Unreal Engine, Marvelous Designer, Maya

During my internship at DNEG in London, I worked with four other interns to create a cinematic in Unreal Engine. We recorded motion capture performances and retargeted the animation onto MetaHuman characters with mentorship from DNEG artists.

My primary contribution was creating Midas's custom clothing in Marvelous Designer and skinning it in Maya, building on the Jerusalem MetaHuman workflow. I also gained hands-on experience with motion capture, retargeting, Unreal Engine, and virtual production.
